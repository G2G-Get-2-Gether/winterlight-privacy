Winterlight – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Winterlight is a Christmas hidden-object game. We respect your privacy, and your children's.

What we collect

Nothing. Winterlight does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

What stays on your device

Your progress (best times, completed scenes) and whether you have unlocked the Christmas Village Pack are saved only on your own device, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Christmas Village Pack" is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your payment details. A parental gate protects the purchase screen.

Children

The game is suitable for children and collects no data from them. It contains no ads, no external links and no social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
